Kenya was the fourth country in our summer 2005 fieldtrip program. It was for Jean baptiste Senoble and myself our very first trip to Africa, so we were very curious and impatient to arrive there! Our main objective on this expedition was to visit the ruby mining areas in the country. But things changed a little bit after our arrival due to Jean Baptiste special attraction for emerald green and his discovery of Kenyan famous green garnet: Tsavorite. I've to say that resisting to the green garnet was an impossible task as I was then "surrounded" by Tsavorite enthousiasts like Kennedy Khamwathi and Campbell Bridges. So besides ruby, tsavorite became a familiar gem and it was worth discovering it! This trip was very different from the other field trips we use to have as this time I had no friends in the area... In order to help us I asked the support from[ ICA](http://www.gemstone.org/) vice president Jean Claude Michelou and ICA Ambassador to Kenya Suzie Kennedy as several months that I became a member of ICA (International Colored stones Association).  ICA help was terrific: Suzie and Kennedy her husband were waiting for us at the airport and helped us like never we were help in a fieldtrip: Before our arrival they contacted all the Kenyan miners to help us to organise our visits. More, Suzie husband: Kennedy Khamwathi decided to come with us as he wanted also to visit all these mines and miners. Nothing could help us more and everywhere people were present, aware of our arrival and very welcoming! I've to say that these few days meeting Kenyan miners were very impressive and we have learn a lot from them. I would like here to thanks them for their welcome and their time.  Currently Kenyan miners needs more exposure to the market, more promotion about their stones. They are mainly searching for partners willing to work with them. If after your visit to these pages you are interested to contact them, I can of course help you but a better way could be to [contact the ICA](mailto:ica@gemstone.org) and ICA Ambassador Suzie Kennedy... You can consult the [report we send to ICA](ICA05.php) about our Kenyan trip and also read in the Dec 2005 issue of the "Revue de gemmologie AFG" the article I've written after this trip.
